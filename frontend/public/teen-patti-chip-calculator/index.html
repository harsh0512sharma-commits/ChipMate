Teen Patti Chip Calculator & Boot Manager

Calculate Teen Patti chip values, boot stack distributions, and fast rebuy packs for 3-Patti home games. Keep blind rounds flowing smoothly without chip shortages.

Why Teen Patti Requires Unique Chip Calculations

Teen Patti has a very different chip velocity than Texas Hold'em. In poker, only two players post blinds each hand. In Teen Patti, every single player posts a boot amount before every hand. In a 7-player game, 7 chips enter the pot on every deal, meaning small-denomination chips circulate at triple the speed of a poker table.

If you don't calculate your chip tray properly, the table will run out of small chips within 45 minutes, stalling the game while people exchange bills or make change.

Recommended Chip Structure for Home 3-Patti

For a standard Diwali or weekend game with ₹100 or ₹200 buy-in packs and ₹5 or ₹10 boots:

Chip Color Recommended Value Purpose in Teen Patti
White ₹5 or ₹10 Boot Pot & Blind Bets (Keep at least 150 chips in bank)
Red ₹25 or ₹50 Chaal & Double Chaal rounds
Blue / Green ₹100 or ₹200 Sideshows, Deep Showdowns & Large Pots
Black ₹500 High Rebuy Reserves (kept in the Host Bank)

Standardized Rebuy Packs for Fast Play

To avoid pausing the game every time a player busts out, hosts should pre-bundle standardized Rebuy Packs. When a player says "rebuy", the host taps 1 button in ChipMate and hands them an identical pre-counted bundle:

  • Standard ₹500 Pack: 10 Whites (₹100) + 8 Reds (₹200) + 2 Blues (₹200).
