ChipMate – Poker & Teen Patti Settlement Calculator

Throw away the spreadsheet. Never argue over chips again.

ChipMate is the authoritative, 100% free real-time chip ledger and debt settlement engine engineered for Texas Hold'em poker and Teen Patti home games. Designed specifically for home game hosts and private poker clubs, ChipMate eliminates the chaos of late-night mathematical calculations, missing chips, and heated post-game arguments.

What Problems Does ChipMate Solve?

  • Real-Time Buy-In & Rebuy Tracking: Keep track of every player's initial buy-in and subsequent rebuys in real time. No forgotten sticky notes or disorganized WhatsApp messages.
  • Physical Chip Bank Vault: Configure custom chip denominations (White, Red, Blue, Green, Black) or equal chip values. The bank vault prevents ghost chips from entering the table and ensures table chip inventory matches the physical box.
  • Borrowed & Lent Chips: Seamlessly record chips borrowed between players or host credit without corrupting table balance.
  • Mathematical Zero-Sum Verification: Before settling, ChipMate mathematically verifies that Total Buy-Ins == Total Cash-Outs. If a chip is missing, you know immediately before anyone pays.
  • Automated Debt Minimization: Instead of having 8 players make 12 confusing cross-payments, our bipartite settling algorithm consolidates all net balances into the fewest possible direct peer-to-peer transfers.
  • 1-Tap WhatsApp Settlements: Dispatch beautiful, clean settlement summaries with player UPI IDs directly to your group chat in one click.
  • Zero App Required for Guests: Only the host needs an account. Friends join as guests in 1 tap or view the live read-only ledger in their mobile browser without downloading an app or signing up.
