How to Settle a Home Poker Game: The Host's Complete Checklist

Follow this proven 6-step checklist to wrap up your home Texas Hold'em or Teen Patti cash game in minutes, ensuring zero missing chips and seamless UPI settlements.

1. The 15-Minute Pre-Settlement Warning

Never end a game abruptly. Announce "Three more orbits" or "Final hand in 15 minutes." This gives short stacks time to push or prepare their cash-out, and prevents players from complaining that they didn't have time to recover losses.

2. Lock the Buy-In Ledger

As soon as the final hand is dealt, announce that the bank is closed. No further rebuys or chip exchanges are allowed. Having an open bank while counting chips is the fastest way to create an accounting discrepancy.

3. Stack Chips in Clean Denominations

Have each player stack their chips in stacks of 10 or 20 by color. This makes verification fast and transparent to everyone at the table.

6. Dispatch to WhatsApp & Verify UPI Receipts

Tap "Share via WhatsApp" to post the formatted settlement instructions into your group chat. Have players execute their UPI transfers on the spot before leaving the venue.
